JunctionShare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Developer: FutureStack Services
App: JunctionShare (com.junctionshare.app)
Contact: hello@rydio.app

JunctionShare is a last-mile carpool helper for people around Princeton Junction and West Windsor, New Jersey. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • No JunctionShare accounts or in-app payments in the current release.
  • No advertising SDKs and no sale of personal information.
  • The name and WhatsApp number you save stay on your device. They are not uploaded to a JunctionShare server.
  • This release does not request device location. Nearby riders in the inbox are demo data.
  • Contacts are read only after you tap the contacts button and pick one contact. Cancelling the picker reads nothing else.
  • Destination search sends the text you type to Photon (Komoot) or, when configured, Google Places. The map preview loads Google Maps.
  • Continuing a match opens WhatsApp (wa.me), which is operated by Meta under its own policies. There is no in-app chat and no WhatsApp alert service in this release.

Information stored on your device

If you use Profile, the app may store locally in the device secure store (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore):

  • Display name
  • WhatsApp phone number, saved when you leave the field or pick a contact

These values are not uploaded to a JunctionShare backend in the current MVP (the app does not operate an account server for profiles). Clearing the phone field deletes the saved number. An active carpool request (destination and note) is also stored on the device until you end it or the time window expires.

Location

This release does not request location permission and does not read your device position. Nearby riders shown in the inbox are demo cards built into the app so you can try the flow. If a later release uses location while a request is active, this policy will be updated.

Contacts

JunctionShare asks for Contacts permission only when you tap “Use number from this phone” on Profile and then choose one contact in the system picker. The app reads the phone number on the contact you picked. If you cancel the picker, no contacts are read. The app does not scan your address book. Contacts are not uploaded to JunctionShare servers.

Destination search and maps

When you search for a destination, the text you type is sent to Photon at photon.komoot.io (operated by Komoot). If the build is configured with a Google Maps API key, the same query is sent to Google Places instead. The in-app map preview loads Google Maps. “Open in Google Maps” hands the place to the Google Maps app or your browser. Those companies process the query under their own terms and privacy policies. Coordinates are attached to a request only after you pick a suggestion.

WhatsApp handoff

When you choose to continue in WhatsApp, JunctionShare opens a wa.me link to the other person’s number with a short prefilled greeting. Message content is handled by WhatsApp/Meta, not by JunctionShare servers. This release does not send WhatsApp alerts and has no in-app chat.

Trip notes and nearby activity

Short trip notes and nearby request cards exist to support matching. There is no public social feed in the app. Block/report tooling noted in the UI may arrive in a later release.

Children

JunctionShare is intended for adult commuters and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 18 where a higher age is required for the store listing).

Data retention and deletion

On-device profile data remains until you clear the field, clear app data, or uninstall. Android removes it on uninstall. The iOS Keychain can keep it after uninstall; JunctionShare deletes those keys when it detects a fresh install. Because profile fields are not stored on a JunctionShare server in this MVP, there is no cloud profile to delete with us. For questions, email hello@rydio.app.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves (for example, when live matching backends launch). The effective date above will change when we do.
