JunctionShare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Developer: FutureStack Services
App: JunctionShare (com.junctionshare.app)
Contact: hello@rydio.app

JunctionShare is a last-mile carpool helper for people around Princeton Junction and West Windsor, New Jersey. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • No JunctionShare accounts or in-app payments in the current release.
  • No advertising SDKs and no sale of personal information.
  • Profile details you enter (name, WhatsApp number, opt-ins) stay on your device.
  • Location is used only with your permission to help match nearby ride requests.
  • Continuing a match opens WhatsApp (or wa.me), which is operated by Meta under its own policies.

Information stored on your device

If you use Profile, the app may store locally using the device secure storage:

  • Display name
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Your choices to share location while requesting and to use WhatsApp for handoff

These values are not uploaded to a JunctionShare backend in the current MVP (the app does not operate an account server for profiles).

Location

With your permission, JunctionShare may access approximate or precise location while a carpool request is active so it can show nearby matching opportunities within a short radius (about 100m–1km). You can decline location permission; some matching features will be limited. The current release may also show demo nearby riders while live matching is rolled out.

Contacts

If you choose the contacts helper on Profile, the app may read contacts only to help you fill your WhatsApp number. Contacts are not uploaded to JunctionShare servers.

Destination search and maps

When you search for a destination, the search text may be sent to a geocoding provider (Google Places if configured, otherwise Photon by Komoot) to return place suggestions. Map previews may load map tiles or links from Google Maps. Those providers process the query under their own terms and privacy policies.

WhatsApp handoff

When you choose to continue in WhatsApp, JunctionShare opens WhatsApp or a wa.me link with a prefilled message. Message content and the phone number you use are handled by WhatsApp/Meta, not by JunctionShare servers.

Trip notes and nearby activity

Short trip notes and nearby request cards exist to support matching. There is no public social feed in the app. Block/report tooling noted in the UI may arrive in a later release.

Children

JunctionShare is intended for adult commuters and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 18 where a higher age is required for the store listing).

Data retention and deletion

On-device profile data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Because profile fields are not stored on a JunctionShare server in this MVP, there is no cloud profile to delete with us. For questions, email hello@rydio.app.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves (for example, when live matching backends launch). The effective date above will change when we do.
